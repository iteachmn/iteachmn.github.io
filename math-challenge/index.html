Features

Why kids & parents love it

📖

Lesson Mode

Four lesson units — addition, subtraction, times tables and division — taught with animation and voice. Learn the times tables by singing along, karaoke-style.

6 Practice Modes

Addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, mixed and random — 5 levels, and you choose the question count.

Stickers & Streaks

Earn sticker rewards after every session — 23 to collect, plus daily streaks that keep kids coming back.
