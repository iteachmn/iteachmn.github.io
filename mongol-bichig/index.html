How to use

Getting started

  1. Choose your language

    On first launch, pick the app's language. This also sets the helper script shown next to the traditional letters (Latin transliteration, or Cyrillic if you choose Mongolian). You can change it later in Settings.

  2. Pick a course on Home

    Start with Name if you just want to write your name, or Reading / Writing to learn the script properly.

  3. Work through a lesson

    Read the rule, meet each new letter, practise, then take the confirmation test. Passing unlocks the next lesson.

  4. Trace with your finger

    Follow the faint model from top to bottom. Words are traced in one piece, exactly as their letters join when written.

  5. Write your name (My Name)

    Type your name in Latin or Cyrillic to see it in traditional script. Adjust any letter if you prefer a different spelling, then trace it and save it.

  6. Keep practising

    Use the Practice tab for drills that focus on your weak letters, and the Alphabet tab whenever you need to look a letter up.

About the script

Written top to bottom

Traditional Mongolian script is written in vertical lines from left to right. A letter changes shape depending on whether it sits at the start, middle or end of a word — the app shows you every form.

Letter order, example words and grammar notes follow the Mongolian national textbook Үндэсний бичиг VI (2023) and the Mongolian Academy of Sciences guide to written Mongolian (2023).

FAQ

Questions

Do I need to speak Mongolian?

No. If you choose a language other than Mongolian, the app shows Latin transliteration and word meanings alongside the script.

Does it need an internet connection?

Almost everything works offline. The one exception: when you enter a name that isn't in the built-in dictionary of more than 15,000 words, that text is sent to a conversion service. Offline, the app falls back to a built-in approximation.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, and your progress is stored only on your device.

Is it free?

All learning content is available without payment. The app shows ads.
